# Known failures and guardrails

- **Old loader shuffle (research failure):** a 1024-item buffer over class-sorted manifest produced severe batch label-order bias and fold collapse. Full-dataset shuffling corrected the mechanism.
- **Derivative-heavy training (research/data-quality failure):** derivatives can dominate patient/slice counts and are not trusted for generalization. Keep `include_derivatives: false`.
- **Historical complete CV (research failure):** pre-shuffle patient OOF ROC-AUC was 0.544640 with fold collapse.
- **Sealed-test transfer run (evaluation boundary):** patient AUC 0.538462 with all patients predicted Stone; gates failed. The sealed test is permanently invalidated for tuning and must not be reopened.
- **Development transfer guard (current champion):** KS 0.397516 and threshold positive-rate delta 0.236025 failed despite OOF gates passing.
- **Incomplete run 20260828_183238_763200:** missing top-level metrics/artifact files; do not report it as a complete experiment.
- **Test backend mismatch (implementation/infrastructure):** two smoke tests assumed NumPy-returning tensors under the torch backend. Fixed with backend-neutral conversion; no metric changed.
- **E004 final-retrain transfer collapse (research/evaluation guard):** the seed-43 CV candidate reached patient OOF ROC-AUC 0.854032, but its locked 15-epoch all-development retrain produced probability std 0.103676, KS vs OOF 0.726708, and a threshold positive-rate delta 0.503106; all 161 development patients were positive at the locked threshold. Candidate rejected; E000 remains BEST.
- **E006 epoch-1 final-retrain transfer collapse (research/evaluation guard):** the same E004 candidate at a one-epoch full-development retrain produced probability std 0.047622, KS vs OOF 0.447205, and threshold positive-rate delta 0.447205; only 4.97% of development patients were positive at the locked threshold. This brackets a severe final-protocol score-regime failure rather than a simple late-epoch-only issue.
- **E006 diagnostic wrapper omission (implementation):** the first transfer invocation lacked the fixed E004 OOF patient-prediction CSV required by the guard. The artifact was copied from the already-completed E004 development-only run and the guard rerun; no model retraining, labels, or sealed-test access were added.
- **E007 BN recalibration (research failure):** three shuffled clean-development passes over E005's fixed weights corrected the locked-threshold prevalence shift (delta 0.031056) and produced adequate spread (std 0.433687), but the resulting bimodal score distribution remained far from OOF (KS 0.322981 > 0.15). Do not promote this post-processing method without an independent transfer-stable result.
- **E008 one-pass BN recalibration (research failure):** reducing clean-statistics recalibration from three passes to one changed little (std 0.435253, threshold delta 0.031056, KS 0.316770). The BN-only direction is not accepted as a transfer fix.
- **E009 equal fold ensemble (near-miss):** averaging all five E004 fold-best checkpoint probabilities restored spread (std 0.313090) and threshold prevalence (delta 0.018634), but KS vs OOF remained 0.173913, narrowly above the <0.15 gate. Do not call this a verified improvement; test only the pre-specified robust median aggregation next.
- **E010 median fold ensemble (research failure):** median aggregation worsened the transfer shape (KS 0.242236 versus equal-mean 0.173913) despite passing spread and threshold gates. Equal mean remains a near-miss only.
- **E011 patient-balanced weak-fold diagnostic (research failure):** equalizing patient frequency on fold 2 improved patient AUC only 0.680147→0.683824 while reducing slice AUC 0.553994→0.539090; the best epoch stayed 1. Do not spend a full-CV budget on this sampler without new evidence.
- **E012 independent ensemble-subset audit (research failure):** the omit-fold-0/omit-fold-3 transfer near-passes seen in seed 43 did not repeat with seed-42 E000 checkpoints; all-five and both subsets failed KS. Do not select fold subsets based on the seed-43 development guard.
- **E013 external interruption (execution/resource boundary):** Windows user logoff at 05:41:14 terminated the active seed-44 CV after folds 0 and 1 and part of fold 2. The log ended without a traceback, and no complete OOF result was produced. Preserve the partial run for provenance; recover only the missing folds with the same seed/config/splits in E014.
- **E014 seed-44 CV (research failure):** the recovered canonical CV completed with OOF patient ROC-AUC 0.804603, only 0.000463 above E000 and 0.049428 below E004. OOF gates passed, but fold 0 was near chance at patient AUC 0.555147 and the result did not improve the champion; no final retrain was launched.
- **E015 cross-seed ensemble (near-miss transfer failure):** averaging corresponding E004/E014 fold-best predictions raised patient OOF AUC to 0.871486 and the worst fold to 0.8125. Transfer spread and threshold prevalence passed, but KS vs OOF was 0.180124, above the strict 0.15 gate. Treat the ensemble as a research near-miss, not a deployable or BEST model.
